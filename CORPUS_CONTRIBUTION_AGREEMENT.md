# Audio-V Validation Corpus Contribution Agreement

Version 1.0 — 2026-07-26

This agreement applies only to recordings, source masters, metadata, and related validation material submitted to the Audio-V Validation Corpus (“Corpus”). It is separate from the Audio-V software Contributor License Agreement.

## 1. Contribution

“Contribution” means each submitted sound recording, musical composition embodied in it, source master, stem, metadata record, provenance statement, artwork, and other accompanying material. “Project Maintainer” means DRAZY, the Audio-V maintainer.

## 2. Contributor representations

By submitting a Contribution, You represent that:

1. You are at least 18 years old and have authority to enter this agreement.
2. You own or control the rights needed for both the sound recording and the underlying composition, or the composition is in the public domain.
3. You have obtained any performer, producer, label, publisher, sample, and other permissions required for the rights granted below.
4. The provenance and technical information You provide is accurate to the best of Your knowledge.
5. The Contribution does not knowingly infringe another party’s copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, contract, or other rights.

## 3. Rights grant

You grant the Project Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable, and sublicensable license to:

- reproduce, store, archive, checksum, inspect, decode, measure, and analyze the Contribution;
- encode, transcode, resample, filter, dither, clip, limit, alter channels, truncate, corrupt, repair, and otherwise create validation derivatives;
- distribute the Contribution and its validation derivatives in complete or excerpted form;
- publish metadata, provenance labels, hashes, measurements, benchmark results, and technical documentation;
- use the Contribution in public and private test, calibration, demonstration, research, commercial, and noncommercial corpora; and
- sublicense a public Corpus release under the specific open-data license recorded when You submit the Contribution, such as CC BY 4.0 or CC0 1.0.

This grant does not transfer Your copyright ownership.

## 4. Attribution and publicity

You may select attribution by legal name, artist name, contributor identifier, or anonymity. Audio-V may factually identify the Contribution as Corpus material but will not imply Your endorsement of an Oracle Engine verdict or of Audio-V.

## 5. Corpus integrity

The Project Maintainer may reject, quarantine, or remove a Contribution from future Corpus versions if its rights, provenance, or ground-truth label becomes disputed. Previously published open-license copies may remain usable under the license that accompanied them.

## 6. No obligation and no warranty

The Project Maintainer is not required to include or continue using a Contribution. Except for the representations in Section 2, the Contribution is provided without warranty.

## 7. Acceptance record

Acceptance must be recorded in the Corpus manifest with:

- contributor or authorized representative;
- agreement version;
- acceptance date;
- attribution choice;
- permitted public Corpus license;
- source-master SHA-256; and
- a stable contribution identifier.

Electronic acceptance is effective. A maintainer must retain the acceptance record outside the public repository when it contains personal information.

> This project document is designed to establish explicit validation rights. It should receive legal review before Audio-V publicly redistributes contributed recordings.
